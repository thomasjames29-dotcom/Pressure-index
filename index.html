How to read this

Pressure is a transparent 0–100 heuristic from shots, shots on target, corners, dangerous attacks and possession, adjusted for match minute. It describes attacking activity in the available stats; it is not a calibrated goal-probability forecast. API-Football may not provide every statistic for every fixture. The higher the risk band, the stronger the attacking surge and the greater the probability of an imminent goal.
